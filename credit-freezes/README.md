Scope. All 20 CFPB directory freeze-tagged entries, including LeasingDesk and Teletrack referrals and unresolved SageStream coverage, plus two prescreen controls. Related entries do not establish independent requests. The 2025 PDF has 19 freeze-company entries; the live directory adds a LeasingDesk tag.
